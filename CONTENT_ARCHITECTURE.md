# Content Architecture

- **Main page:** `/` (the game). Every page links to it and ends with "Play Shadow Shift free".
- **Support pillar:** How to Play (basics) → Features (what exists) → Gameplay Tips (mastery).
- **Blog cluster:** genre-level informational articles (beginner's guide, timing, parkour movement,
  obstacles) that link back to the pillar and the game.
- **Trust pages:** About, Contact, Privacy, Terms (low sitemap priority).

**Article template:** breadcrumb (Home / Blog / Article), H1, published date + reading time,
featured image, body with H2/H3 and contextual links, "Keep reading" box (related articles, a
supporting page, the game), CTA.

**Content rule:** every claim about Shadow Shift matches the code (speeds, durations, controls).
No made-up statistics, reviews or authors; the author and publisher are the "Shadow Shift"
organisation.
