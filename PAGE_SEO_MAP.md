self canonical, `robots: index, follow, max-image-preview:large`, Open Graph + Twitter tags with a

`article:modified_time`. There is no FAQ schema, because Google limits FAQ rich results to
authoritative sites. The keyword targets are hypotheses until the Phase 4 research.
