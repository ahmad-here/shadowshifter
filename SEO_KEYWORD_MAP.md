**What the portals do well:** instant play, category pages, a short description per game, huge
domain authority. **What they lack:** in-depth player guides. Single-game sites (eParkour,
rooftoprun.net) rank on an exact-match domain plus a short page.

**Strategy:**

1. Own the brand query ("shadow shift game", "shadow shift parkour").
2. Win long-tail player questions with real guides.
3. Get listed on the portals (CrazyGames, Poki, itch.io, Game Jolt): that is where head-term traffic
   actually goes, and their pages link back.

## Search-intent rules

- Informational articles answer the question in the first paragraph, then go deeper, and only
  mention Shadow Shift where it's a genuine example.
- Navigational pages (game, how to play) lead with the action (play, controls).
- No doorway pages, no keyword stuffing, no AI filler: every article must be useful even to someone
  who never plays the game.
