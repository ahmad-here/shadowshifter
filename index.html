Shadow Shift: 3D Parkour Game

Shadow Shift is a free 3D parkour game that you play online, right in your browser, on desktop or mobile. There is nothing to download or install. Free-run across neon rooftops, leap the gaps between towers, carve round corners at full speed, run along walls, climb onto taller buildings and slide under pipes, all while dodging red lasers that burn on contact.

Game features

  • Rooftop parkour and free running: wall-runs, climbs, vaults and slides
  • An endless runner that speeds up the further you get
  • Black portals that plunge the city into darkness, and mirror portals that reverse every control
  • Works offline once loaded, on any modern browser with WebGL2

How to play

  • Jump: arrow up, W, Space or swipe up
  • Slide: arrow down, S or swipe down
  • Turn corners, steer in the air and wall-run: arrow left or right, A or D, or swipe sideways
  • Pause: Esc or P
