How to Play Shadow Shift

Shadow Shift is an endless rooftop runner: your runner sprints forward on their own and speeds up over time. Your job is to read the route ahead and pick the right move at the right moment.

01

How to start playing

Open the game page and press Start run (or Space). There is nothing to install and no account. The game runs in any modern browser with WebGL2, on desktop or mobile.

03

Movement

Jumping

Buildings float apart, with gaps of a few metres between them. Jump just before the edge: any jump from the edge reaches the next building, even at top speed. Running off an edge without jumping is a fall. Some buildings sit higher or lower than the one you are on: small rises must be jumped, drops are simply landed on.

Sliding

Slide to pass under overhead pipes. Pressing slide in mid-air slams you down faster and slides on landing.

Turning and sideways moves

When a building ends with another one beside it, press left or right at the corner and the runner carves round it on the ground. You can also turn in mid-air during a jump. Where your lane ends beside a parallel one, press toward it to hop across.

Wall-running and climbing

Some gaps are too long to jump but have a wall on one side: jump, then press toward the wall while in the air to run along it. Run or jump into the face of a much taller building to climb it and pull up onto the roof.

04

Obstacles and hazards

  • Red laser gates: jump them. Touching a laser burns you.
  • Pipe bundles with cyan bands: slide under them.
  • A single pipe with an amber valve: jump or slide, either works.
  • Rooftop AC units: vaulted automatically, no input needed.
  • Trampolines: bounce on the glowing pad, then press left or right in mid-air to steer onto the side building.
05

Portals

Mirror portals turn the runner around for 10 seconds and reverse every control: jump and slide swap, and so do left and right. Black portals bring 8 seconds of darkness; survive it and the game runs for you for a few seconds.

06

Restarting

After a fall or crash, press Space or tap the button to run again. Each run builds a new city. Your best distance is saved in your browser.

07

Beginner tips

  • Jump late, at the edge, rather than early.
  • Let the obstacle colour decide: red jump, cyan slide, amber either.
  • On a dead-end building, press the turn early: the game holds it until the corner.

08

FAQ

Is Shadow Shift free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads or purchases.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Swipe up to jump, down to slide and sideways to turn.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser; it needs WebGL2, which all current browsers support.

Why does the screen go dark?

You ran through a black portal. The darkness lasts 8 seconds; watch the glowing edges of the route.
