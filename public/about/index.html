About Shadow Shift

Shadow Shift is a free, single-player 3D parkour runner that plays in the web browser.

01

The game

It is an endless runner: the runner sprints forward on their own and the player decides when to jump, slide and turn. The longer you last, the faster it gets, and the score is simply the distance run.

02

The rooftops

The game takes place on the rooftops of a neon city at night. Buildings of different shapes and heights float apart, and each run builds a new city, so no route can be memorised.

03

The moves

Beyond jumping and sliding, the runner carves round corners, runs along walls, climbs taller buildings, bounces on trampolines and hops between lanes, while lasers, pipes and two kinds of portal test their reactions. See every feature.

04

In the browser

The game is written in TypeScript with React and Three.js (WebGL2), and runs entirely in your browser: no server, no account, no download. Your best distance and shard total are stored only on your own device, and once loaded the game also works offline.

05

What comes next

The game is actively developed: movement, animation and new route types are being improved. Feedback is welcome on the contact page.
