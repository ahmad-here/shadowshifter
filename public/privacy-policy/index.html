Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

01

What we collect

Nothing. Shadow Shift has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party scripts, and sends no gameplay data to any server.

02

What is stored on your device

The game stores your best distance, your total energy shards, your settings (sound, graphics quality, reduced effects) and which tips you have seen, in your browser's local storage. It also caches the game's files so it can run offline. This data never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

03

Hosting

The site is hosted by Wasmer. Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and requested pages) under its own policies.

04

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Data on your own device is under your control as described above.

05

Changes

If the game ever adds analytics or other data collection, this page will be updated first.
