Inside the game

Explore Shadow Shift Game Features

Every feature below is in the game today.

01

3D rooftop running

Square, hexagonal, triangular and wedge-shaped buildings float above a neon city at night. The runner sprints on their own and accelerates from 10 to 19 metres per second, so the same route gets harder the longer you survive.

02

Jumping between buildings

Gaps of a few metres separate the buildings, and buildings sit at different heights: rises to jump up onto and drops to land on. The route is generated so that a jump from any edge always lands on the next building, at every speed. Falling is always a timing mistake, never bad luck.

03

Wall-running

Some gaps are too long for any jump, with a tall wall along one side. Jump, then step toward the wall in mid-air: the runner plants their feet on it, runs along it and kicks off onto the next building.

04

Climbing

Some buildings are far taller than the one before. Run or jump into the face and the runner climbs it and pulls up onto the roof.

05

Sliding under obstacles

Overhead pipe bundles with cyan bands must be slid under. A single pipe with an amber valve can be jumped or slid. Rooftop AC units are vaulted automatically.

06

Laser hazards

Red laser gates stretch across the roof at knee height. Jump them: touching a beam burns the runner.

07

Branching paths and special routes

  • Connected corners you carve round at full speed.
  • Forks offering a side branch or the straight route; both rejoin.
  • Trampolines that launch you high enough to steer onto a side building.
  • Parallel lanes you hop between.
08

Mirror portals

Run through one and the runner turns around for 10 seconds, with every control reversed: jump and slide swap, and so do left and right.

09

Black portals and darkness

A black portal plunges the city into darkness for 8 seconds. Only the glowing edges of the route stay easy to see. Survive it and the game runs for you for a few seconds as a reward.

10

Endless and fair

Every run is a new, randomly generated city. Amber energy shards are collected along the way and counted per run and in total. Your best distance is saved in your browser.
