Shadow Shift Gameplay Tips and Strategies

Advice built on how the game actually works. New to the game? Start with how to play.

01

Jump late, not early

A jump from the very edge of a building always reaches the next one, so there is no need to jump early. Jumping too soon is the most common way to fall short.

02

Read colour, not shape

Red means jump, cyan means slide, amber means either. At top speed there is no time to study the obstacle, so let the colour decide.

03

Time slides to the pipe, not the building

A slide lasts a little under a second. Start it just before the pipe, not as soon as you see it, or it ends too early.

04

Press turns early

On a dead-end building you can press left or right early: the game holds the press and turns at the corner. Pressing early is safer than pressing late.

05

Wall-runs need two inputs

Jump first, then press toward the wall while in the air. A jump alone falls into the gap.

06

Trampolines: steer during the bounce

A normal jump cannot reach the side building. Land on the pad, then steer in the air.

07

Forks: pick a side early

At a fork both branches rejoin, so neither is a trap. Decide early and commit.

08

Mirror portals: think "opposite"

For 10 seconds, up means slide and left means right. Many players find it easier to think "opposite" before every press.

09

Darkness: follow the glowing edges

In the darkness after a black portal, the neon edges of the route stay lit. Watch them, not the buildings. Survive the 8 seconds and the game runs for you briefly.

10

Use mid-air steering to fix mistakes

Turns also work in the air. If you jump and realise the route turns, press the direction straight away.
