How to Beat Your High Score in Endless Runner Games

Your high score is not beaten by one brilliant run. It is beaten by many runs that avoid the same mistakes.

Published 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

01

Play for consistency, not records

Chasing a record makes you tense and risky. Aim to play cleanly instead; long runs follow from clean play.

02

Find where you fall

After each run, name what ended it: an early jump, a missed turn, a slide too soon. Most players fall to the same two or three mistakes. Fix those first.

03

Warm up

Your first run is rarely your best. Treat the first two or three as warm-up and do not judge them.

04

Prepare for the late game

Runners speed up, so the late game is a different skill. In Shadow Shift the run reaches 19 metres per second; look further ahead and decide earlier as the speed rises.

05

Take breaks

After several short failed runs in a row, stop for a minute. Tired eyes react later, and frustration leads to early presses.

06

Use what the game gives you

Learn every helper: Shadow Shift holds early turn presses on dead ends, lets you steer in mid-air, and catches a ledge after a jump that only just falls short. Knowing these lets you play safer.

07

Next steps

Sharpen your timing, then go for a new best.
