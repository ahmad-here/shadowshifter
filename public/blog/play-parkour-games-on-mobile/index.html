How to Play Parkour Games on Mobile

Runner games are made for phones: short runs and four directions. A few habits make swipe controls feel as precise as a keyboard.

Published 2026-11-16 · 4 min read

01

The usual swipe layout

Swipe up to jump, down to slide, left or right to turn or change lane. Shadow Shift uses exactly this, and a swipe down in mid-air slams you to the ground.

02

Swipe short and fast

Games detect a swipe from a short, quick movement. Long, slow drags can register late or not at all. Flick your thumb about a centimetre and lift it.

03

One swipe per action

Lift your thumb between swipes. Starting a new swipe before the last one ends can be read as one diagonal movement.

04

Hold the phone steady

Use both hands in landscape or rest the phone on something in portrait, and swipe with one thumb from the centre of the screen. Starting near the edge can trigger the system's back gesture.

05

Get a smooth frame rate

  • Close other tabs and apps.
  • Use the game's quality setting if it stutters: Shadow Shift has Auto, Low, Medium and High, and Auto lowers quality when frames drop.
  • Play fullscreen to avoid accidental browser gestures.
