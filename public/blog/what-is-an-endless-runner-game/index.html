What Is an Endless Runner Game?

An endless runner is a game where your character runs forward automatically and the level never ends. You do not steer the speed; you only decide when to jump, slide or turn. The goal is simply to last as long as you can.

Published 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

01

The four ingredients

  1. Automatic running: the character always moves forward. Your input is about timing, not movement.
  2. Rising speed: the run gets faster the longer you survive, so difficulty grows without new rules.
  3. Generated levels: the route is built as you go, often randomly, so it cannot be memorised.
  4. A distance or points score: every run ends in failure; the question is how far you got.
02

Why the genre works

Runs are short, so a failure costs seconds, not minutes. The controls are few, so anyone can start. And because the route is new every time, the hundredth run still asks for attention. That mix of quick restarts and steady improvement is what keeps players coming back.

03

2D, lane-based and free 3D runners

  • 2D side-scrollers run left to right and are usually one-button jump games.
  • Lane runners run into the screen and let you switch between a few fixed lanes.
  • 3D parkour runners add real geometry: gaps between buildings, height changes, corners and moves such as wall-runs and climbs.

Shadow Shift belongs to the third group: it runs across separate floating rooftops, speeds up from 10 to 19 metres per second, and scores you by distance.

04

What makes a good one

Fairness above all: every obstacle readable in advance and every gap possible. Then variety, so the route keeps surprising you, and feel, so every jump and landing is satisfying on its own.
