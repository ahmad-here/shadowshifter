How to Avoid Obstacles in Running Games

Obstacles are what turn running into a game. Avoiding them is less about fast fingers and more about reading early and choosing the right move. Here is a method that works in almost any running game.

Published 2026-11-16 · 6 min read

01

Step 1: Learn the obstacle language

Running games are designed to be readable. Each obstacle type has a look that tells you the answer, usually through colour, height or shape:

  • Low obstacles are jumped.
  • High obstacles with a gap underneath are slid under.
  • Obstacles in the middle often allow either.

In Shadow Shift the colour gives it away: red laser gates are jumped, pipe bundles with cyan bands are slid under, and a single pipe with an amber valve can be either jumped or slid. Once you know the language, you no longer study each obstacle; you just react to its colour.

02

Step 2: Spot obstacles as early as possible

Look ahead to the next building, not at your character. Good games help here: Shadow Shift raises each building and its obstacles into place well ahead of you, so nothing appears at the last moment. Use that time.

03

Step 3: Decide, then wait

Choose the move as soon as you see the obstacle, then wait for the right moment to press. The most common mistake is pressing too early: a slide that starts too soon ends before the pipe, and a jump that starts too soon comes down on the laser.

04

Step 4: Match the timing to the move

  • Jumps peak in the middle, so start them a little before the obstacle.
  • Slides last a fixed time; start them just before the obstacle reaches you.
  • Automatic moves (such as vaults over rooftop units) need no input at all; do not press anything.
05

Step 5: Handle combinations

Obstacles near a gap or a turn are harder because two decisions arrive together. Handle them in order: what is closest first. If a jump leaves you in the air near a turn, many games, including Shadow Shift, let you turn in mid-air.

06

Step 6: Recover after a near miss

A near miss is a good sign: your timing was close. Don't tense up. Breathe, look ahead again and treat the next obstacle as new.

07

Special hazards

Many runners add hazards that change the rules for a while. Shadow Shift has two portals: a mirror portal reverses your controls for 10 seconds, and a black portal brings 8 seconds of darkness where only the glowing edges of the route are easy to see. The trick for both is the same: slow your thinking down, follow what you can see, and press deliberately.
