Parkour Game Terms: A Glossary for Players

Parkour games borrow words from real parkour and from game design. Here are the terms you will meet most, in plain words.

Published 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

01

Movement

Precision jump
A jump to a small, exact landing spot.
Gap jump
A jump across the space between two surfaces, such as rooftops.
Vault
Crossing a waist-high obstacle using your hands. Often automatic in games.
Slide
Dropping low to pass under an obstacle while keeping speed.
Wall-run
Running along a vertical wall for a few steps, usually to cross a long gap.
Climb-up
Pulling yourself onto a higher surface.
Ledge catch (ledge grab)
Grabbing an edge you only just failed to land on.
Slam
Forcing a fast drop from the air, often into a slide.
Flow
Chaining moves without losing speed.
02

Game design terms

Coyote time
A short moment after leaving an edge when a jump still works.
Input buffer
A press made slightly early that the game remembers and performs on time.
Hit-stop
A tiny freeze on impact that makes a crash or hit feel heavier.
Procedural generation
Levels built by rules at runtime instead of by hand.
Near miss
Passing an obstacle by a very small margin; many games reward it.
Telegraphing
Showing a hazard clearly in advance so the player can react.
03

Runner terms

Auto-run
The character runs forward without input.
Lane switch
Moving sideways between parallel paths.
Run
One attempt, from start to fall.

Most of these appear in Shadow Shift: it has vaults, slides, wall-runs, climbs, a ledge catch, slams, coyote time, an input buffer and near-miss rewards.
