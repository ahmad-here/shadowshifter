How to Play 3D Games in Your Browser Without Downloading

Modern browsers can run real 3D games: no installer, no store, no account. Here is how it works and how to get the best experience.

Published 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

01

How it works

Browsers expose the graphics card through WebGL, a standard way for web pages to draw 3D. WebGL2, supported by every current major browser, is enough for detailed scenes with lighting, shaders and animated characters. The game is downloaded like a web page, so it starts in seconds.

02

Which browsers work

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all support WebGL2, on desktop, Android and iOS. Shadow Shift supports all four.

03

If it runs slowly

  • Hardware acceleration: make sure it is on in your browser settings.
  • Other tabs: close heavy tabs, especially other games and video.
  • Quality setting: lower it in the game's settings.
  • Laptop power: plug in, or choose a performance power mode.
  • Update: an up-to-date browser and graphics driver fix most problems.
04

Is it safe?

Browser games run inside the browser's sandbox and cannot install anything on your device. Shadow Shift has no accounts and no analytics; scores and settings stay in your browser (see the privacy policy).

05

Offline play

Some browser games, including Shadow Shift, cache themselves after the first visit and keep working without a connection.
