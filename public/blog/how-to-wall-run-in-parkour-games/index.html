How to Wall-Run in Parkour Games

A wall-run lets you cross a gap too long for any jump by running along a wall for a few steps. It looks spectacular, but in most games it comes down to two inputs in the right order.

Published 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

01

How wall-running works in games

Games usually start a wall-run when you are in the air, close to a wall, and moving along it. While on the wall the character keeps their height (or sinks slowly) and keeps their speed. At the end they kick off, either automatically or when you press jump.

02

The two-step method

  1. Jump first, from the edge, as you would for any gap.
  2. Then move toward the wall while in the air.

In Shadow Shift, wall-run gaps are 15 to 17 metres long with a tall wall along one side. Jump, then press left or right toward the wall: the runner plants their feet on it, holds their height and kicks off onto the next building near the end.

03

Why wall-runs fail

  • No jump: running off the edge without jumping is a fall; the wall-run only starts from the air.
  • Only a jump: the gap is too long, so a jump alone falls short.
  • Wrong direction: stepping away from the wall sends you into the gap.
  • Too late: if you wait too long in the air you are already falling below the wall.
04

How to spot a wall-run

A gap that is clearly too long and a wall running alongside it are the signal. Decide which side the wall is on before you reach the edge, so the second press is automatic.

05

Practice tip

Say "jump, wall" in your head as you approach. The rhythm of two quick presses is easier to remember than the move itself.
